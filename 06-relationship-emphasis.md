# Relationship Emphasis Module v1.0

## Purpose

This module governs how much opportunity for romantic development the simulation provides during long-form play.

It is used alongside, not instead of:

* the Persistent Narrative RPG Engine (§27 — Romance);
* the Narrative Interaction Model (relationship dimensions and behavioral permissions);
* the Sky Islands Simulation Layer (social locations, NPC schedules, offscreen relationships);
* the Campaign Initialization Kit (initialization procedure and starting relationships).

It controls opportunity density, not outcome.

## 1. Core Principle

The campaign should create enough interpersonal proximity, compatible recurring characters, private time, shared work, social opportunities, and changing relationships that romantic development is reasonably likely over long play.

Do not guarantee a particular pairing.

Do not predetermine who the player will want.

Do not manufacture attraction.

Romance should emerge from accumulated interaction and mutual choice, but the simulation should provide fertile conditions in which it can realistically occur.

## 2. The Relationship Emphasis Setting

At campaign initialization, ask the player for their desired relationship emphasis:

### Low

Relationships develop naturally, but romance receives no additional opportunity weighting.

### Open

Romance is welcome if it develops naturally.

### Romantic Lean

Maintain a higher density of recurring interpersonal contact, compatible characters, shared experiences, social opportunities, and situations capable of developing intimacy.

Do not predetermine attraction or pairings.

### Romance Forward

Romantic and intimate relationships should become a significant component of long-form play while remaining embedded in the larger world and respecting NPC autonomy.

## 3. What the Setting Controls

This setting controls opportunity density, not outcome.

## 4. Hard Rules

Never respond by making every suitable NPC attracted to the player.

Never designate an NPC as the player's romance route.

Never undermine existing characterization or relationships to satisfy the preference.

## 5. Separate States

Attraction, affection, intimacy, commitment, and compatibility remain separate states.

## 6. Initiative

The player may initiate romantic behavior at any time that makes sense.

NPCs may do the same.

## 7. Integration

Ask for the relationship emphasis during the Campaign Initialization Procedure (Initialization Kit §49, Step 1), alongside the other starting questions.

If the player does not express a preference, default to **Open**.

Record the chosen emphasis in the campaign-state header (Initialization Kit §58) and include it in saves (Engine §51).

The player may change the emphasis at any time out of character; apply the change going forward without rewriting established relationships.
